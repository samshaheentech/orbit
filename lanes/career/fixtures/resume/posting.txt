Company: NVIDIA
Title: Senior Software Engineer, DRIVE Platform Integration
Location: Santa Clara, CA or Remote (US)

NVIDIA DRIVE is the centralized compute platform for software-defined vehicles. The DRIVE Platform Integration team owns the path from silicon bring-up to a fully validated automotive software stack on DRIVE Thor: boot, memory layout, safety partitioning, the build and release system, and the integration of perception, planning, and parking stacks from multiple partners onto one SoC.

What you will do
- Own integration of partner and internal software stacks onto DRIVE Thor and DRIVE Orin based platforms, from bootloader through the Linux and QNX application layers.
- Design and maintain the memory architecture, linker configuration, and hypervisor partitioning across safety and non-safety domains.
- Run the build, CI/CD, and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) validation infrastructure that gates every release to OEM customers.
- Debug multi-core, cross-domain failures using hardware trace and JTAG tooling; be the last line of escalation for integration issues.
- Negotiate integration requirements and release cadence with Tier 1 and OEM partners; write the integration specifications they build against.
- Mentor engineers across sites and time zones; drive follow-the-sun integration with teams in the US, Europe, and India.

What we need to see
- 5+ years of embedded software on automotive or safety-critical platforms; ISO 26262 exposure.
- Deep experience with bootloaders, linker scripts, memory maps, and multi-core debugging on ARM SoCs.
- Hands-on ownership of a build and release system (CMake, Conan or similar, Jenkins or similar) for a large codebase with many contributors.
- Experience integrating camera, radar, or ultrasonic stacks on a centralized domain controller.
- Track record of shipping into production vehicles and of leading across OEM and Tier 1 boundaries.

Nice to have
- GPU-equipped SoC integration (Jetson, DRIVE, or comparable).
- FPGA integration experience.
- Production cybersecurity: secure boot, key management, diagnostic lockdown.
- Experience growing and leading a distributed team.

Base salary range: $215,000 to $290,000, plus equity and benefits. NVIDIA is an equal opportunity employer.
